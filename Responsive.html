London

London is the capital city of England. It is the most populous city in the United Kingdom, with a metropolitan area population of over 9 million.

Standing on the River Thames, London has been a major settlement for two millennia, its history spanning from its Roman founding to the present day.

Paris

Paris is the capital and most populous city of France. It is located in the north-central part of the country.

Paris has a rich history, with its origins dating back to the 3rd century BC. It is known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture.

Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital city of Japan and one of its 47 prefectures. It is the most populous metropolitan area in the world, with over 37 million residents.

Tokyo is a major financial center and is known for its modern architecture, shopping, and vibrant culture.
